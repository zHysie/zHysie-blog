    About

    Welcome to Loading Today ✨

    Loading Today is a playful OS-inspired escape built for the CodeTV Hackathon — a soothing, interactive page you can keep open while you work, vibe, or take a tiny digital breather.

    The idea is simple: it shows how much of the day has “loaded” so far — a gentle reminder to pause, look out the window, and take a breath between tasks and tabs.

    Time-based details use your browser’s own timezone, so the clock, status bar, moods, meetings, and day progress follow the device you’re actually using. Local weather and a few location-aware terminal moments may use an approximate IP-based location when available.

    I’m Eric Van Holtz, and I built Loading Today to make the browser feel calmer, cozier, and a bit more alive — with animated backgrounds, environmental and interface sounds, fun interactions, and branded moments that feel like part of the world.

    A browser extension is on the way, bringing Loading Today to your new tab page as a peaceful place to land every time you open a browser window. The extension will also bring the latest news into one spot, so you can catch up, reset, and get back to your day without bouncing all over the internet.

    Interested in sponsoring the project or bringing your brand into the experience? Sponsorships can include a branded theme, custom wallpaper, dedicated app(s), browser home page placement, dock presence, announcements for your brand, products, or services, and more.

    Privacy & Legal

    Loading Today is designed to feel cozy, not creepy. We collect as little as possible and keep most of your settings on your own device.

    Local time

    The clock, status bar date, day progress, moods, meetings, and other time-based moments use your browser’s timezone setting.

    Approximate location

    Some features may look up your approximate city and coordinates using your IP address via third-party geolocation services. This powers local weather and a few location-aware terminal details. The approximate location result is cached in your browser for up to six hours. If lookup fails, those location-aware features fall back to Detroit, Michigan — where Loading Today was built.

    Weather data

    Weather is fetched from Open-Meteo using your approximate coordinates. Results are cached in your browser for up to an hour.

    Local storage

    Preferences such as your chosen theme, wallpaper, ambient-audio and interface-sound settings, clock style, and window positions are saved in your browser’s local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you clear it.

    Cookies

    We set one functional cookie (loading-today-info-intro-seen) so the welcome window doesn’t open every time you return. It lasts up to one year and contains no personal information.

    Third-party embeds

    Some features load content from other services — for example YouTube (Code TV) and Tenor (terminal memes). Those services may set their own cookies or collect usage data under their own policies.

    Optional clock typefaces and the Notes handwriting font are loaded from Google Fonts when used. That request may share your IP address with Google under Google’s privacy policy.

    Analytics

    We use Fathom Analytics to understand aggregate traffic — page views, referrers, and general usage patterns. Fathom is cookie-free, does not track individuals across sites, and does not sell data. We do not use advertising trackers.
